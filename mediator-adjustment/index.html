Mediator adjustment

Compare the total treatment effect with a model that holds a post-treatment intermediate response fixed.

Should you adjust for a mediator?

Not when the target is the total effect. A mediator lies on a pathway from treatment to outcome, so adjusting for it blocks part of the effect the total-effect estimate is meant to include.

Try it in the experiment

  1. Start with baseline-health adjustment and a true total effect of 3.
  2. Include the intermediate response in the outcome model.
  3. Remove it and compare both estimates with the total-effect target.

A direct effect is a different estimand, not a repaired total effect. Simply adding a mediator to a regression does not generally identify a direct effect; mediator–outcome confounding and treatment–mediator interactions require additional care.
